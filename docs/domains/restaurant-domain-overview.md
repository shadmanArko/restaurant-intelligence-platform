# Restaurant Domain Overview

Version: 1.0
Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This document defines the business domains of the Restaurant Operating System.

The domain model represents how the business actually operates.

Technology must adapt to the domain.

The domain must never adapt to technology limitations.

---

# Core Business Objective

Convert raw ingredients into profitable customer experiences while maintaining complete operational and financial visibility.

# Key Business Concepts

---

## Expected Consumption

What SHOULD be consumed.

Derived from:

Recipe
×
Orders

---

## Actual Consumption

What WAS consumed.

Derived from:

Inventory Movements

---

## Variance

Expected Consumption
vs
Actual Consumption

Variance is a key operational metric.

---

## Food Cost

Actual ingredient cost required to produce menu items.

---

## Cost Of Goods Sold (COGS)

Actual inventory value consumed by sales.

---

## Waste

Inventory consumed without generating revenue.

---

## Customer Lifetime Value

Total value generated by a customer.

---

## Operational Intelligence

Understanding:

- Waste
- Margin
- Efficiency
- Customer Behavior
- Supplier Performance
